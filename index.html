Precision Engineering for Flawless Textiles

At Jade-Textile, our plant features state-of-the-art industrial dyeing machinery engineered for high-capacity and uniform textile treatment.

By utilizing advanced liquor circulation systems and automated pressure-temperature controls, our machinery ensures exceptional dye penetration, preventing uneven spots and guaranteeing vibrant, long-lasting color brilliance across all fabric batches.
